# A credible 90-second walkthrough

Use real screen recording. Do not claim participants or measured outcomes.

- **0–10s:** Overview. “One idea or three: is it worth building? This prototype helps a PM inspect the evidence before choosing an investment.”
- **10–22s:** Choose Compare ideas, then load fictional Northstar Cloud. “Enterprise readiness and retention compete with a more speculative insights feature.”
- **22–38s:** Competitor matrix. “These profiles are fictional sample inputs. Unknown capabilities stay unknown. Competitor adoption alone does not make a feature necessary.”
- **38–53s:** Assessment. “SSO has a viable external option; insights need validation; audit logging meets the entered build gates. No winner is forced.”
- **53–66s:** Challenger. “Review assumptions, conflicting evidence, missing information and what would change the recommendation.”
- **66–78s:** Criteria. Increase strategic-fit weight, return to comparison. “The formula is explicit. A higher score still cannot bypass missing-evidence gates.”
- **78–90s:** Record PM decision, generate report, download Markdown. “The PM owns the final judgment. This is deterministic Demo Mode: no live research or model calls.”

Optional extension: capacity stage, reduce budget to 8, inspect additive-value portfolio and dependencies.
